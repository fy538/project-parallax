# Parallax constructivist style: non-architectural test

The previous organic/relational/systemic range test became too reductive: flat symbols with a shared palette, but little of the depth and authored construction in the original Parallax visual reference. This test returns to `tools/ai-video/style-references/r15_neutral_default.png` as a **style reference only** and changes the subject completely. The imagined AI behavior is reward hacking: the count of planted saplings rises while the broader forest remains depleted.

The built-in image-generation tool made an initial illustration and one hierarchy edit. Project assets: `remotion-templates/public/style-lab/constructivist-forest-score-v1.png` and `constructivist-forest-score-v2.png`. The chosen v2 is registered as `constructivist-forest-study` under **Experiments** in Remotion Studio; source: `remotion-templates/src/experiments/ConstructivistForestStudy.tsx`. Renders: `remotion-templates/out/style-lab/constructivist-forest-study.png` at 1920×1080 and `constructivist-forest-study-thumbnail.png` at 320×180. The illustration is unlettered and still; no narration or motion was tested.

**What works:** the planted plot, depleted surroundings, and soil/root cutaway make a particular argument rather than an interchangeable icon. Layered strata, diagonals, silhouettes, and selective fine roots retain the original constructivist depth. Enlarging the plot in v2 made it readable at thumbnail size. The gold rising trace gives the visual claim a clear counterpoint.

**What remains weak:** the image borrows the original reference's broad circular backdrop and sweeping left-to-right field too literally. The rising gold trace reads as a score only with surrounding context; without narration, it could just mean progress. Root detail is useful in a held frame but becomes texture at thumbnail size. This is a better direction for a non-architectural scene, not yet proof of a complete illustration system.
