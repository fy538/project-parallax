# Parallax illustration language: range test

Three illustration-only frames test whether the quieter constructivist direction can cover more than architectural metaphors. All were generated once with the built-in image-generation tool, using `promise-transfer-constructivist-v2.png` and `understanding-grid-constructivist-v2.png` as **style references only**. No typography was included in the images. The Remotion board places them at equal size over the same paper color.

**Verdict.** The palette, flat cuts, right-weighted crop, and lone gold accent make a coherent visual family, but do not yet make the illustrations unmistakably Parallax. The most promising shared device is the **shaped absence**: the seam between two selves, the unsupported space under a shared burden, and the channels between repeated parts. The next refinement should make that absence carry a specific argument, rather than adding texture or more geometry. Keep these as exploratory candidates, not production defaults.

## Exact image-generation prompts

**Organic.** Input images: `promise-transfer-constructivist-v2.png` and `understanding-grid-constructivist-v2.png`, both style references only.

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: illustration-only visual-language test for Parallax, 16:9 editorial video. Both attached images are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY, not edit targets. Explore the ORGANIC member of this same visual family, for a philosophical question about whether a self remains continuous through change. Create a distinct, memorable image from TWO broad flowing cut-paper contour silhouettes, suggestive of a human profile or body in motion but without facial features or literal portraiture. One contour is slightly displaced from the other so a clean irregular ivory absence appears between them; a single small muted-gold wedge marks the divergence. Give the form a graceful curve and a decisive angular cut so it still belongs to constructivist editorial modernism. Put the entire subject mainly in the RIGHT HALF; the LEFT HALF is quiet open warm ivory. Same restrained palette as references: ivory, warm charcoal, walnut, sand, one muted gold accent. About 4–6 large shapes total, hard deliberate edges, matte nearly-solid fills, strong negative space. No faux paper grain, scratch marks, soft gradients, shadows, fine hairlines, architecture, grids, bridge, transfer line, brain, eye, robot, tree, logo, text, numerals, pseudo-text, or watermark. This must be legible at video thumbnail size and work as an illustration WITHOUT a title.
