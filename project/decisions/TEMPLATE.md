# NNNN — Short imperative title (Active voice)

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** (single name for solo project)

## Context

What forced this decision? What constraints, signals, or prior choices created the situation? Keep this section factual; the *why now* should be self-contained for a reader six months later.

## Decision

The chosen path. One paragraph max. State it directly — no hedging.

## Alternatives considered

- **Alt A:** brief description, why rejected.
- **Alt B:** brief description, why rejected.

## Consequences

What this commits us to. What it prevents. What becomes easier or harder. What signal would tell us this decision was wrong (so we know when to revisit).

## References

- Links to relevant docs, prior ADRs, external articles.
