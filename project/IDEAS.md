## Shorts Series Candidates

| Series Name | Concept | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Framework in 45 Seconds** | One philosophical/analytical framework, applied to one geopolitical example. Standalone, shareable. | Philosopher's Lens direction |
| **History Rhymes** | A single striking historical parallel to a current event. No explanation — just the juxtaposition. Let the viewer connect the dots. | Time Collapse direction |
| **Both Sides Are Wrong** | 45-second Dialectic: state the popular debate, then the reframe. Drives traffic to full Dialectic episodes. | Dialectic direction |
| **What Happens Next?** | Wargame-style: present a scenario, ask "what would you do?", reveal the decision tree in the next Short. | Wargame direction |
| **The Market Says...** | "Kalshi prices [event] at X%. Is the market right?" 45-second breakdown of one prediction market question — state the price, explain why it might be wrong, invite the viewer to decide. Drives traffic to long-form analysis. | Oracle + Prediction Market layer |
| **Was I Right?** | Revisit a past prediction or Kalshi market. Show what you said, what the market said, what actually happened. Score yourself publicly. Builds trust through accountability. | Oracle direction (quarterly long-form + periodic Shorts) |
