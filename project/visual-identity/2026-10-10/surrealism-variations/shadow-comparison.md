# Shadow scene style comparison

The same encounter in two visual treatments: the child's and shopkeeper's real hands remain apart while their wall shadows connect. The constructivist version preserves the earlier scene's composition and adapts the palette, figures and contrast to Parallax's existing visual language.

The latest constructivist refinement keeps the bread's broader painted shapes and restores featureless faces. Facial detail comes from broad tonal planes and head contours only: no eyes, eyebrows, nose, mouth or other facial features. Three constructivist versions are shown below alongside the earlier blue illustration.

## Latest refinement: painted bread and featureless faces

The loaf uses broad ochre planes and graphic scoring marks. The faces use anonymous angular painted forms, with a few tonal planes providing depth. Clothing details, composition and the connected wall shadows remain intact.

## Assessment

The constructivist version is the stronger fit for the current Parallax identity. Its high contrast gives the shadows greater presence, and the angular people preserve the established tone. The surreal contradiction remains easy to read.

The comparison supports developing surreal relationships within the existing visual language. The detail refinement gives clothing and hands more specific construction while retaining the strong silhouettes. The latest edit brings the bread closer to the surrounding illustration and defines the blank faces through painted planes. Surface pattern on the counter remains substantial, so the detail hierarchy still deserves attention in future scenes.
