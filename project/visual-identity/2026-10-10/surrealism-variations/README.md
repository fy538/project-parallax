# Surrealism variations for Parallax

Five variations explore how space and shadows can carry an idea within the same child and shopkeeper encounter. All use warmer ink-drawn people, geometric painted surroundings and a restrained blue, ivory and copper palette. The strongest visual ideas in this set are independent shadows and continuous changing surfaces; quiet architecture provides a more restrained everyday baseline.

## Compare the five variations

| Variation | Visible idea | Possible narrative use | What needs development |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Quiet impossible architecture | A warm encounter within skewed geometric planes | Everyday scenes with a subtle sense of uncertainty | The spatial contradiction is understated; the human drawing is currently more distinctive than the surrealism. |
| 2. Folded space | The counter rises into a fold beneath the loaf | An ordinary relationship becomes difficult or changes its apparent structure | The result depicts a physical fold more clearly than the simultaneous viewpoints proposed in the prompt. |
| 3. Monumental distance | An oversized room and long counter separate the people | Distance, exclusion, scale or an obstacle to connection | People become smaller and less intimate; reserve the scale change for a meaningful moment. |
| 4. Independent shadows | The real hands remain apart while their shadows meet | A possible interpretation alongside an observable action | The narration must establish what the shadow represents; it should not silently assert an unseen motive as fact. |
| 5. Continuous changing surfaces | Counter, wall and window frame become one surface | A change of viewpoint reinterprets the same structure | Reduce decorative texture and make the continuity equally readable in motion. |

## 1 Quiet impossible architecture

The closest everyday extension of the earlier architectural study, with more specific, attentive people. The counter and wall create an unusual junction, but the surrealism remains subtle.

## 2 Folded space

The counter bends upward into a conspicuous architectural fold, placing the loaf on an unexpected plane. This is the most graphic spatial interruption in the set.

![Surreal shop with a folded counter](s02-folded-viewpoints.png)

## 3 Monumental distance

The familiar exchange occupies a vast, almost empty room. The long counter enlarges the emotional distance without changing the actual people into symbolic objects.

![Child and shopkeeper separated by a long counter in a monumental room](s03-monumental-distance.png)

## 4 Independent shadows

The people hesitate in the foreground while their shadows connect on the wall. The contradiction is immediately readable and leaves the real human action intact.

![Separated real hands with connecting shadows on the wall](s04-independent-shadows.png)

## 5 Continuous changing surfaces

One blue plane runs from the counter up the wall and around the window. The architecture changes role while retaining its material identity.

![Counter continuing into the wall and window frame](s05-continuous-surfaces.png)

## Direction to develop

Use the warm human drawing as a starting point, then introduce one purposeful spatial or shadow contradiction when the argument needs it. The independent shadows offer the strongest immediate storytelling idea; the continuous surface offers a promising recurring way to reveal relationships. Keep quieter scenes available so the surreal moments retain their force.

The people are more emotionally present than the earlier mannequins, though the small facial features and nostalgic clothing still pull parts of the set toward storybook illustration. A further refinement could use more contemporary clothing, less surface grain and a less uniformly gentle expression while preserving the observed gestures.

For motion, first test a simple human gesture within the quiet scene. Separately test a shadow connection and a surface reveal with authored layers so their meaning stays exact. These stills are flattened PNGs; editable components would have to be prepared for that workflow.
