## Generated sample gallery

Each study interprets the same bread shop encounter: a child with a coin and an adult behind the counter. Staging, figure design and palette vary, so compare their visual character; a controlled animation comparison would require matching those elements first.

### 1 Modern woodcut

Strong ink shapes and broad carved marks. The closest continuation of the existing direction.

![Modern woodcut bread shop study](01-modern-woodcut.png)

### 2 Layered paper theatre

Matte cutout forms and shallow stage depth. The sample is a flattened PNG; separate layers would be needed for authored layer animation.

![Layered paper theatre bread shop study](02-paper-theatre.png)

### 3 Charcoal with spot color

Searching contours and selective human detail. A more intimate, observational direction.

![Charcoal and spot color bread shop study](03-charcoal-spot-color.png)

### 4 Editorial gouache

Broad painted forms and warm storytelling. The adult's resting hands need correction before an animation trial.

![Editorial gouache bread shop study](04-editorial-gouache.png)

### 5 Graphic risograph

The refined version uses indigo, vermilion and bone with larger shapes and more empty space.

![Refined graphic risograph bread shop study](05b-graphic-risograph.png)

The first attempt below leaned toward vintage textured illustration. It is retained so the change is visible.

![First risograph bread shop study](05-two-color-risograph.png)

### 6 Modernist cel animation

Flat coral and cyan fields, exaggerated profiles and skewed geometry. This version makes the graphic animation language explicit, with a pronounced retro cartoon character.

![Modernist cel animation bread shop study](06-modernist-cel.png)

### 7 Conceptual line drawing

Spare navy pen contours and two small red accents. The continuous counter and apron contour suggests a vocabulary for connecting ideas; meaningful line transformations would be developed in animation.

![Conceptual line drawing bread shop study](07-conceptual-line.png)

### 8 Architectural surrealism

Anonymous painted figures, long shadows and opposing counter angles. The room carries the strangeness while posture carries the human encounter.

[Five further surrealism variations with warmer ink-drawn people](surrealism-variations/README.md).

![Architectural surrealism bread shop study](08b-architectural-surrealism-flat.png)

### 9 Ink wash with crisp figures

Cool atmospheric washes and more sharply defined people. This version retains observed garment and hand detail against a looser room.

![Ink wash with crisp figures bread shop study](09-ink-wash-crisp-figures.png)

### 10 Drawn surreal collage

Engraved figures within cut architectural fragments and a broad red plane. The current sample emphasizes the printed surface and cut boundaries; the spatial rearrangement is comparatively restrained.

![Drawn surreal collage bread shop study](10-drawn-surreal-collage.png)

### 11 Rotoscope inspired editorial drawing

Selected ink contours, painted planes and a more active human gesture. This is a generated illustration study; no live-action footage was traced or restyled to make it.

![Rotoscope inspired editorial bread shop study](11-rotoscope-inspired-editorial.png)

### 12 Pinscreen inspired tonal worlds

Soft luminous profiles emerge from dark monochrome fields. The simplified version replaces the photographic detail of the first attempt with broad sculpted forms.

![Pinscreen inspired tonal bread shop study](12b-pinscreen-tonal-world-simplified.png)

### 13 Paint on glass

Dragged cobalt and white paint, scraped edges and a small warm accent. The image explores a visibly revised painted surface; transformations would need a separate motion trial.

![Paint on glass bread shop study](13-paint-on-glass.png)

## Additional animation references

Two further references could inform movement or occasional sequences within a broader identity:

- **Bauhaus theatre:** geometric bodies and spatial choreography. [Schlemmer and the Triadic Ballet at Bauhaus Archiv](https://www.bauhaus.de/en/sammlung/highlights/214_buehne/379).
- **Direct film abstraction:** line, color and rhythm for transitions or abstract passages. [Begone Dull Care by Norman McLaren and Evelyn Lambart at NFB](https://www.nfb.ca/film/begone_dull_care/).

## Combinations to consider

The leading new combination is expressive ink-drawn people inside geometric, subtly impossible spaces, with conceptual line drawing for explanations. Paper theatre remains a useful alternative to compare visually. These are candidates for further samples and motion tests; a final identity has not been selected.
