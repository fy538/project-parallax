# Face-rule audit of the twelve `ai-care-motives` stills

> 12 October 2026. Every head in the episode's stills, enlarged and checked against rule 1 of [PICTURE_RULES.md](../../PICTURE_RULES.md). The stills were made on 7 and 8 October, before the rule was written down.
>
> **Question.** Does the face rule apply to episode 1's existing stills, and if so what would it take?
> **Sheets.** `heads.jpg` (every close head) and `raters.jpg` (the ten figures in `aigen-06`). Images stay out of git; `tools/qa/contact_sheet.sh` remakes the crops.
> **Status.** Explored. Tiger has not ruled.

## Result

Eleven of the twelve stills breach the rule somewhere. Only `aigen-11` (the building) has no person in it. About 24 heads are involved.

| Kind of breach | Where | How it reads at viewing size |
|---|---|---|
| Reads as a face part | `aigen-08`, both figures: a dark band across the eye line and a nose wedge. `aigen-06`, rater 8: glasses | As a face. These are the clear ones |
| Nose point on a profile | `aigen-01` both people, `aigen-03`, `aigen-04` both people, `aigen-07` (the outline steps at nose and lips), `aigen-06` raters 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10 | As a faceted profile. It is the house style of these stills |
| Centre ridge on a front view | `aigen-02` shopkeeper, `aigen-09`, `aigen-06` rater 6 | As a split of light and shadow. Borderline |
| A line inside the ear only | `aigen-02` child, `aigen-05`, `aigen-10`, `aigen-12`, and most heads above | Not noticeable without enlarging |

Passes outright: `aigen-06` rater 4 (front view under a fringe).

## What follows

- Bringing every head under the rule is a re-illustration of the episode, not a touch-up.
- The three heads that read as a face part are a small, bounded fix: `aigen-08` (on screen about 12 seconds) and the glasses in `aigen-06` (on screen about 70 seconds).
- The profile wedge is what the new look's head design has to solve anyway (critique, experiment 1).
