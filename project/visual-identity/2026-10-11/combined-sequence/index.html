Where the synergy lives.

The painting gives the scene its material character. Remotion controls where attention goes: the cut, the shift in scale, each drawn leader, the labels and the diagram’s reading order.

One fixed paper field carries all four beats. The cup becomes a recurring visual anchor while the explanation changes around it.

What to judge.

Does the cut feel purposeful? Do the annotations clarify the image? Does the diagram still belong to the same world?

The cup has more rendered material detail than our faceted people. That consistency question remains open. This study tests the integration and pacing; it does not settle the whole identity.
