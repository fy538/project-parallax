Use case: illustration-story.
Asset: one isolated painted object on a genuinely transparent background, for independent animation in Parallax.
Subject: a single small ceramic teacup, viewed slightly from above in three-quarter view. A round open rim, an oval dark interior, a thick ochre body tapering gently toward a stable foot, and one simple loop handle at the right. Its rim, inner opening, foot and handle have coherent ceramic geometry. No saucer or other objects.
Medium: mature editorial gouache, matte opaque ochre, bone highlights and walnut/ink shadow planes. Model its volume with a few unequal broad planes and two or three selected brush-edge details. Make the cup feel observed and hand painted, with subtle asymmetry, rather than a perfect icon or a photorealistic product shot. Plain surface, no logo, writing, pattern or ornament.
Center the full cup with generous transparent margin. No ground plane, cast shadow, backdrop, checkerboard drawn into the image, glossy glaze, reflections or 3D render finish.
