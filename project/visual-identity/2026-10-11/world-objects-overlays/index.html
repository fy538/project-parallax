What this proves

A small, usable visual system.

The paintings were generated with the built-in image tool. Camera movement, object placement, contact shadows, SVG overlays and chart animation were authored and rendered in Remotion. This batch demonstrates compositing and controlled motion; it does not test AI image-to-video animation or prove that water, cup interiors or scenery can move independently.

The scenery files are flattened paintings. The cups have generated transparency and were checked on both light and dark fields. The cutaway is an intentional fantasy, not a factual diagram. The chart contains synthetic values solely to compare layout and contrast.

Watch the combined 26-second Remotion sequence: ordinary cup → impossible interior → analytical annotations → diagram on matching paper. It is picture only, with optional draft narration cues. Scenery and recorded narration remain further integration tests.
