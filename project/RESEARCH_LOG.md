## 1. Professor Jiang Xueqin Analysis

### Who He Is
- Chinese-Canadian educator, Yale undergrad, teaches at Moonshot Academy (high school in Beijing — NOT a professor)
- YouTube channel "Predictive History," 2M+ subscribers
- Uses structural historical analysis, game theory, and Asimov's "psychohistory" concept

### Why He Exploded
1. **Prediction-confirmation loop:** May 2024 "Iran Trap" lecture predicted Trump win, US-Iran war, US loss. First two came true. Video went from handful of views to 680K+. Gained 100K subs in 3 days.
2. **Psychological moment:** In chaotic geopolitical times, audiences crave narrative coherence. Jiang offers the structure that events are patterned and comprehensible.
3. **Cross-cultural arbitrage:** Chinese face, flawless American English, Yale credential, analyzing US decline from Beijing = inherently provocative and shareable from any political angle.
4. **Controversy flywheel:** Criticism generates more searches/views/debate.

### Controversies
- Weaves in Illuminati, Freemason, "Pax Judaica" conspiracy theories
- Critics argue he functions as CCP propaganda asset ("East Rising, West Declining" narrative)
- Mehdi Hasan interview (March 2026) pressed him on antisemitic undertones
- "Professor" title is misleading — he's a high school teacher
- Predictions rely on selective analogies and undemonstrated game theory

### Lessons for Our Project
- **Take:** Market exists for cross-domain pattern recognition content. Enormous appetite.
- **Avoid:** Hard predictions (fragile), conspiracy territory, declarative certainty, unearned academic credentials
- **Our edge:** Actual formal analytical frameworks (Bayesian, game theory) demonstrated visibly, not just name-dropped

## 3. Visual Strategy Research (CRITICAL FINDINGS)

### The Five Visual Styles in This Space
1. **Minimalist 2D (Kurzgesagt):** Clean vectors, bold colors. Signals "educational." 24M subs.
2. **Comic/Cartoon (OverSimplified):** Character-driven humor. 8M+ subs. Too comedic for our tone.
3. **3D Simulation (AiTelly/Kings and Generals):** Realistic environments. Labor-intensive. Signals "military/tactical."
4. **Map/Data Driven (CaspianReport/PolyMatter):** Animated maps, motion graphics, stock footage. The default in geopolitics.
5. **Cinematic AI Illustration (emerging):** AI-generated painterly images with subtle animation.

### Evidence-Based Verdict: AI Illustration is NOT Viable as Primary Visual

**Key data points:**
- Consumer trust drops ~50% when content is perceived as AI-generated
- Only 26% of consumers prefer AI-generated content (down from 60% in 2023)
- 70% lower audience retention for AI-heavy content vs human-fronted
- No successful educational channel found using AI illustration as primary visual
- AI illustration in geopolitics space is associated with disinformation networks (DFRLab found 20+ channels with 2B views mixing real news with fabricated events)
- YouTube terminated 16 major AI channels (35M combined subs) in Jan 2026
- "AI slop" was Merriam-Webster's 2025 Word of the Year

**What IS working:**
- Clean motion graphics + animated maps + stock footage (Wendover, PolyMatter, CaspianReport)
- Narrative-first, visuals-second approach (every successful channel)
- Vibrant illustration + data-driven content hybrid (Kurzgesagt)
- Educational content gets 42% retention — highest of any YouTube category — driven by storytelling quality, not visual spectacle

### Revised Visual Recommendation
- **Core:** Clean motion graphics, animated maps, data visualizations, stock footage integration
- **AI role:** Use as compositing tool (reference art, backgrounds, textures) — never raw AI output as final visual
- **Three-mode grammar:** Historical segments (warm tones), philosophical/analytical segments (clean/cool), contemporary segments (high-contrast modern)
- **Consistency:** Reusable templates, standardized color palette, recurring visual motifs

## 4. TTS/Narration Research

### Current State of TTS (2026)
- ElevenLabs v3 (Expressive) is market leader: natural pauses, breathing, emotion, zero tone drift
- Other strong options: Fish Audio (best value), Murf AI, Inworld TTS
- Voice cloning: can create custom voice from short sample, nearly indistinguishable from original speaker
- YouTube explicitly allows AI voiceovers if content meets originality/authenticity standards

### Evidence Against Pure AI Narration
- 35% viewer drop-off within 45 seconds for AI narration vs human
- Stock AI voices (e.g., ElevenLabs "Josh") increasingly recognizable — triggers "AI content" perception
- For authority-dependent content (philosophy, analysis), human voice carries trust signals AI doesn't replicate well

### Decision: Narrate Yourself
- Human narration is strongly supported by evidence for this content type
- Voice cloning remains an option for scaling later (clone your own voice, not a stock voice)
- Batch recording (3 episodes in one sitting) minimizes time investment
- Side benefit: improves speaking skills for future consulting/workshops

## 5. YouTube Policy on AI Content (2025-2026)

### Disclosure Requirements
- Mandatory since May 2025: must disclose "meaningfully altered or synthetically generated content" that appears realistic
- **Properly labeled AI content receives normal algorithmic distribution** — disclosure doesn't limit reach
- Undisclosed AI content faces reduced recommendations or removal from Partner Program

### Monetization
- July 2025: "inauthentic content" = mass-produced, template-driven, minimal human creative input
- AI-assisted content (research, thumbnails, audio cleanup, editing) IS allowed if meaningful human involvement
- The line: AI as enhancement (allowed) vs AI as replacement (penalized)

### Our Position
- Scripting: AI-assisted research/drafting, human-rewritten final scripts — no disclosure needed
- Narration: Human (Tiger) — no disclosure needed
- Visuals: AI-generated reference material composited into human-directed motion graphics — no disclosure needed as long as final output reflects human creative decisions
- Never use raw, unmodified AI output as viewer-facing content

### Chinese Platforms (Future Reference)
- China mandated AI content labeling by law (Sept 1, 2025)
- Douyin: semi-transparent overlays required, at least 5% of image height
- Bilibili: voluntary creator declaration system
- Bilibili saw 86% YoY increase in AI-related video uploads — more AI-tolerant market

## 7. Growth Strategy Research (0 to 1K Subscribers)

### Timeline Expectations
- Average: 254 days (8-9 months) to 1K subscribers
- Optimistic: 4-6 months with aggressive optimization
- New geopolitics channels average ~4,066 subscribers/month (but high variance)
- First 15-20 videos are "practice" — expect lower metrics

### Key Strategies
1. **Shorts + long-form combined:** 41% faster growth than single-format. Shorts for discovery, long-form for conversion.
2. **70/30 evergreen/trending split:** Evergreen builds lasting library; trending catches algorithmic momentum.
3. **Cross-platform:** TikTok (2.8-3.15% engagement) -> YouTube Shorts -> YouTube long-form funnel.
4. **Reddit:** Engage genuinely in r/geopolitics, r/AskHistorians, etc. 9:1 participation-to-promotion ratio.
5. **Audio quality is non-negotiable:** 23% of viewers never return to channels with poor audio.
6. **Consistent schedule:** Inconsistent posting = 67% slower growth.

### Biggest Mistakes to Avoid
- Random/unfocused content (algorithm can't categorize you)
- Poor audio quality (more important than video quality)
- Not analyzing retention/drop-off data
- Ignoring Shorts funnel
- Expecting results too fast (first 15-20 videos are practice)

### First Video Strategy
- **Recommended approach:** Timely + evergreen hybrid
- Lead with current event hook (catches search traffic), explain deep historical pattern (provides lasting value)
- Target length: 10-13 minutes
- 60%+ audience retention is the key metric to optimize for
- First 8 seconds are make-or-break (55% of viewers drop in first 60 seconds)

### Candidate First Episode Topics (April 2026)
1. **US-China Semiconductor Geopolitics** (STRONGEST) — Trump-Xi summit + tariffs as hook, 200 years of resource competition as depth
2. **US-Iran Crisis** — airstrikes as hook, 73 years of structural conflict as depth
3. **Ukraine War Year 5** — Napoleonic War parallels, "why wars don't end when you think"
4. **25th Anniversary of 9/11** — how catastrophic events reshape geopolitical orders

### Subscription Triggers (Psychology)
- Future value signal ("this channel will teach me things I want to know")
- Identity alignment ("this content is for people like me")
- Trust in clarity ("I understand what this creator does")
- Explicit subscribe ask after delivering best insight (~70% through video)

### Chinese Audience Differences
- Bilibili core demographic: average age 26, 62% aged 16-35 (younger than YouTube geopolitics audience)

- Edelman: 91% of Chinese public has "confidence in their country" vs 39% of Americans
- Content that resonates in English market (anxiety about institutional decay) needs fundamentally different framing for Chinese audiences — NOT mere translation

## 10. Three Critical Content Gaps (Claude Research, April 2026)

### Gap 1: Philosophical/Analytical Frameworks Applied to Geopolitics
No established channel systematically applies Stoicism, Bayesian thinking, game theory, complex systems theory, or epistemological frameworks to current events. Jiang Xueqin's viral explosion (8M+ views from Tucker Carlson interview) proves massive demand. William Spaniel's surging growth with academic game theory confirms from a different angle. But Jiang is discredited by conspiracy theories; Spaniel is narrowly academic. The credible, rigorous, multi-framework version doesn't exist yet.

### Gap 2: Bilingual Content That Bridges Rather Than Translates
China-focused content is aggressively polarized: pro-CCP voices (often state-supported) vs anti-CCP channels (SerpentZA, Laowhy86). No prominent creator provides genuinely balanced, bilingual analysis synthesizing Western and Chinese analytical perspectives. Academic literature calls for "cross-civilizational negotiation between Western and Chinese epistemic perspectives" — incorporating Tianxia and Yin-yang dialectics alongside Western IR theory — but this exists only in journals, not accessible video.

### Gap 3: Epistemological Self-Awareness
Existing content routinely criticized for oversimplification and determinism (Zeihan's China-collapse predictions since 2010, CaspianReport as "too deterministic"). No channel teaches viewers how to evaluate geopolitical claims, recognize cognitive biases, or assess source quality. Tetlock's superforecasting literature has massive academic credibility but minimal YouTube presence.

### What Existing Content Does Poorly (Community Feedback)
- Too surface-level, too clickbait-driven
- Too Western-centric in framing
- Too reliant on geographic determinism
- Lacks explicit uncertainty modeling
- Lacks multiple analytical frameworks presented simultaneously

## 11. Platform Strategy Research (Claude Research, April 2026)

### Bilingual Channel = Algorithmically Destructive
- YouTube recommendation system operates in separate language ecosystems
- AIR Media-Tech warns mixed-language channels create "can watch but can't belong" communities
- YouTube Help Center advises against mixed-language channels
- Poor retention on dubbed tracks pulls entire channel's algorithmic performance down

### Three-Track Model (Recommended)
- Track 1: English YouTube (15-20 min analytical video essays, weekly)
- Track 2: Chinese Bilibili (adapted, not translated; requires MCN or registered entity)
- Track 3: Short-form discovery (TikTok, Douyin, Xiaohongshu; 30-60 sec clips)

### Key Platform Data
- Shorts: 74% of views come from non-subscribers — critical discovery funnel
- vidIQ study of 5M channels: 12+ uploads/month = 8x faster view growth
- Quality dominates frequency for research-heavy content — CaspianReport, PolyMatter, Kraut all post weekly to biweekly
- YouTube Multi-Language Audio feature: allows adding Mandarin track without splitting channel

### Monetization Benchmarks
- YouTube ad RPM for geopolitics/education: $4-7 (US/UK audiences)
- TLDR News: profitable with 12 FT employees, ~£1M annual revenue from YouTube + Nebula, sub-1M subs
- CaspianReport: estimated $8,000-11,000/month from AdSense alone
- Wendover Productions: likely $15,000-40,000/month across diversified streams

### Year 1 Revenue Projections
- Conservative: $2,200 (AdSense $600, sponsorships $1,000, Patreon $500, Bilibili $100)
- Moderate: $8,000 (AdSense $1,500, sponsorships $4,000, Patreon $2,000, Bilibili $500)
- Subscriber target: 5,000-15,000 YouTube subs (wide range; single breakout video can accelerate dramatically)

### Risk Factors
- Production economics: 30-75+ hours per high-quality 20-min video (traditional estimate; AI pipeline reduces this)
- Creator burnout: 79% of creators experienced burnout, 83% of monetized creators
- Algorithm headwinds: educational content faces "one-and-done" viewing pattern
- Market saturation: 90% of YouTube channels fail, 95% of videos get <1K views, <1% of channels reach 100K subs
