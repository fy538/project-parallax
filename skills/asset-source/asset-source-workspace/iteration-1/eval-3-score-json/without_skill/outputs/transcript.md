## Evaluation Criteria

Each asset was scored across six dimensions relevant to documentary video production:

### 1. **Resolution** (Technical Baseline)
- **High:** 4K+ (3840x2160 or higher)
- **Medium:** 1080p to 4K (1920x1080 to 3840x2160)
- **Low:** Sub-1080p (below 1920x1080)

**Why it matters:** Video production requires pixel headroom for scaling, cropping, and zoom effects without degradation. Documentary-style shooting favors high resolution for maintaining detail during color treatment and effects.

### 2. **Aspect Ratio** (Frame Compatibility)
- **Ideal:** 1.75–1.85 (16:9 widescreen = 1.78)
- **Acceptable:** 1.5–2.0 (minor cropping tolerable)
- **Poor:** <1.5 or >2.0 (significant waste or pillboxing)

**Why it matters:** Parallax targets 16:9 delivery (YouTube standard). Portrait or ultra-wide images require heavy cropping, wasting pixels and potentially losing critical subject context.

### 3. **Subject Relevance** (Content Authenticity)
- **High:** Clear cleanroom or semiconductor fab environment
- **Medium:** Tech/industrial with ambiguous cleanroom signals
- **Low:** Generic tech or misaligned subject matter

**Why it matters:** "The Silicon Trap" narrative centers on manufacturing capability and precision engineering. Authentic cleanroom imagery strengthens geopolitical framing; generic tech photos dilute the argument.

### 4. **Visual Clarity** (Production Quality)
- **High:** Sharp, well-lit, professional grade
- **Medium:** Acceptable but requires minor adjustment
- **Low:** Soft focus, poor lighting, or insufficient detail

**Why it matters:** Documentary demand clarity for authority. Soft or poorly-lit assets undermine credibility and fail to convey manufacturing precision.

### 5. **Duotone Compatibility** (Brand System Integration)
- **High:** Strong tonal separation, clear subject-to-background contrast, detail preserved in shadows
- **Medium:** Works with careful treatment but requires adjustment
- **Low:** Loses critical detail in duotone conversion, flat tones, poor separation

**Why it matters:** Parallax's Meridian system converts images to duotone (dark-mode: amber #E5A544 + rust #C23B22 on ink #1A1A2E; light-mode: oxblood + paper). Images with poor tonal separation flatten during this conversion, reducing visual impact.

### 6. **Geopolitical Framing** (Narrative Power)
- **High:** Strong industrial/manufacturing signals, conveys technical complexity
- **Medium:** Neutral tech signals, works when paired with strong narration
- **Low:** Generic or misleading, requires heavy editorial work

**Why it matters:** Every visual should reinforce the episode's central argument: semiconductor capability as geopolitical power. Images without clear manufacturing signals require excessive voiceover explanation.
