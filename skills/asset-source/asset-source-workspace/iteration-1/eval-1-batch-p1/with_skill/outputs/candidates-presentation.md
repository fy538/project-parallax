## Asset 3: beat2-fdr-1941

**Priority:** P1 (must-have)  
**Type:** Photo (Archival)  
**Treatment:** standard (ink → bronze → amber)  
**Script context:** Historical archival. Wikimedia Commons or Library of Congress preferred. (~12 seconds)

### SOURCING NOTE

This is an archival/historical image. **Stock photo libraries (Pexels, Pixabay, Unsplash) do not reliably carry specific historical photographs at usable resolution.** Recommend direct archival sourcing instead.

**Sourcing pathway:**

1. **Library of Congress** (preferred)
   - URL: https://www.loc.gov/search/
   - Search: "Franklin Delano Roosevelt executive order 1941" or "FDR oil embargo Japan"
   - Expected result: High-res scan of formal signing ceremony, public domain
   - Why: LC holds most comprehensive FDR photograph archive; images are pre-digitized at archival quality

2. **Wikimedia Commons** (fallback)
   - URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/
   - Search: "Franklin Delano Roosevelt" + filter "1941" or "executive order"
   - Expected result: Public domain photographs, often linked to LC source
   - Why: Community-curated, always properly attributed

3. **National Archives** (alternative)
   - URL: https://catalog.archives.gov/
   - Search: "FDR 1941" or "oil embargo Japan"
   - Why: Government records; some images available but lower resolution
