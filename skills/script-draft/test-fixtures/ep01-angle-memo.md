# EP01 Angle Memo — The Silicon Trap

## Named Concept
**The Silicon Trap** — the paradox where both the country imposing technology controls and the country being denied are trapped by the same supply chain. Neither can escape without breaking the system that benefits both. The "trap" is structural, not intentional — it emerges from the extreme globalization that made semiconductors possible.

## Decoder Framing
The standard frame: "Who's winning the chip war — the US or China?" Our frame: "What if both sides are walking into the same trap — and history says this was predictable?" The decoder move is showing the audience the structural pattern (denial → acceleration → mutual damage) that neither side's domestic media discusses.

## Cold Open Approach
**Stakes-shock + paradox.** Open with the TSMC Arizona success story — 92% yield, $165B invested — then immediately reveal it covers only 7% of US chip demand. The viewer's assumption ("reshoring is working") gets complicated in the first 30 seconds. This is a contradiction opener, not a context opener.

## Cross-Domain Connections
1. **1941 oil embargo → Pearl Harbor** — resource denial designed to prevent war triggered it instead. Structural resonance: asymmetric dependence weaponized, use-it-or-lose-it pressure. Where it breaks: oil is consumable, chip capability compounds.
2. **COCOM (1949-1994)** — 45-year technology wall that worked against the USSR. The sobering counterexample. Where it breaks: China is deeply integrated, not economically isolated.
3. **Chess vs. Go** — US targets specific chokepoints (chess captures), China surrounds territory (go's patient encirclement). Neither strategy accounts for the trap both create.

## Emotional Arc
1. **Disorientation** (Beat 1) — the paradox of success that isn't enough
2. **Recognition** (Beat 2) — "this has happened before, and the outcomes surprise"
3. **Empathy** (Beat 3) — seeing China's experience from the inside (卡脖子)
4. **Dread** (Beat 4) — the trap springs shut on everyone
5. **Personal implication** (Beat 5) — "you're already inside this story"

## Visual Arc
**Motif: The supply chain web.** Starts as a clean, efficient network in Beat 1 (the marvel of globalization). Becomes tangled and strained in Beat 3 (as both sides pull on it). Splits into two diverging networks in Beat 5 (bifurcation). The motif can be implemented as a RouteAnimation template with a `progress` parameter.

**Visual-first moments:**
- Beat 2: The 1941 timeline appears 3 seconds before narration begins (the viewer sees dates ticking toward Pearl Harbor before knowing why)
- Beat 4: The chess/go boards appear before the metaphor is introduced verbally

**Counterpoint moment:**
- Beat 3: While narration says "the controls are working," the visual shows China's yield curve climbing
