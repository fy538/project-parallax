Known weaknesses of this run
- **Leakage:** the skill edit that mentions "an example that is easy to verify standing in for cases that are not" was written from the same critique that produced the essay stretch item. That win doesn't count as independent evidence.
- **Baseline was not clean:** subagents inherit CLAUDE.md and repo context; the seeded baseline cited `M-TRANSITION-*` lint names despite being told not to read skills/.
- **Seeded fixture does not discriminate:** the baseline nearly matches. Keep it as a regression test for `script_lint.py`, not as evidence the skill adds value.
- **Essay miss:** with the skill, Lens 10 returned DISCIPLINED and the ending was called "not wishy-washy" (expected: OVER-HEDGED, ending retreats from its position). Either the lens thresholds are lenient or that expectation is arguable — decide before the next run.
- **Cost:** with-skill runs produce ~2.2-2.6x the output of baseline.
