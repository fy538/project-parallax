# EPISODE STRUCTURED BRIEF

---

**EPISODE NUMBER:** 01
**WORKING TITLE:** The Silicon Trap / 硅陷阱
**TARGET LENGTH:** 15-20 minutes
**HOOK:** The US spent three years trying to cripple China's chip industry. China responded by building one. Both sides may end up worse off — and history says this was predictable.
**THESIS:** America's semiconductor export controls have completely failed and China is winning the chip war.
**ANALYTICAL SPINE:** Bayesian updating — what we believed, what happened, what we should believe now.

---

## SECTION 1: NARRATIVE ARC

### Beat 1 — The Hook (0:00–2:30)

**Scene:** Open on TSMC Arizona Fab 1's 92% yield rate. The flagship of American semiconductor reshoring appears to be working. Then the reversal: it produces just 7% of US chip demand. Chips cost 50% more.

**Content:** Establish the paradox. The most expensive foreign investment project in US history is both a technical triumph and a strategic drop in the ocean.

**Key data:**
- TSMC Arizona: $165B total investment, largest FDI greenfield project in US history
- Fab 1 yield: 92%, 4 points above Taiwan counterpart
- But: only 7% of US chip demand; 50% cost premium
- CHIPS Act: $52.7B authorized → $30.9B awarded → only $6B disbursed

---

### Beat 2 — The Pattern: When Empires Weaponize Resources (2:30–7:00)

**Scene:** "This isn't the first time a dominant power tried to deny a rising power access to a critical technology."

**Content:** Two historical parallels.

**Parallel A — The 1941 Oil Embargo**

The US cut off Japan's oil supply. Japan attacked Pearl Harbor. Resource denial always backfires.

*Where the parallel holds:* Asymmetric dependence weaponized. The gap between policy intention and actual outcome.

**Parallel B — COCOM**

17 Western nations restricted technology to the Soviet Bloc. The USSR never caught up. But enforcement was leaky (Toshiba-Kongsberg).

---

### Beat 3 — The Current Chapter: What's Actually Happening (7:00–12:00)

**Content:** The US plays chess; China plays go.

**The US hand:**
- Export controls escalated through three administrations but policy whipsaws
- The revenue-sharing deal: Trump let Nvidia sell AI chips to China for 15% of revenue
- CHIPS Act money barely flowing
- Jake Sullivan's 2022 speech was the inflection point

**China's hand:**
- SMIC produces 7nm chips without EUV — it works
- A prototype EUV machine exists in Shenzhen — the chokepoint strategy will collapse
- DeepSeek proved you don't need advanced chips for competitive AI
- Big Fund III: ¥344 billion

---

### Beat 4 — What We Thought vs. What Happened (12:00–16:00)

Three belief updates showing experts were wrong about everything.

**Update 1:** Controls were supposed to cripple China. They didn't. Huawei profits surged 564%.
**Update 2:** China could never make advanced chips without EUV. SMIC proved this wrong.
**Update 3:** Restrictions wouldn't hurt US companies. Nvidia lost its entire China market.

---

### Beat 5 — The Cliff We're Standing On (16:00–18:00)

The semiconductor supply chain is too complex for anyone to control. Full decoupling harms everyone. BCG says self-sufficiency costs $1 trillion. The question: how fast will AI arrive?

## SECTION 3: HISTORICAL PARALLELS

### PARALLEL A: 1941 Oil Embargo → Japan
Resource denial backfires. The US embargoed oil, Japan attacked Pearl Harbor. Same thing is happening with chips — denial triggers the exact response you feared.

### PARALLEL B: COCOM (1949-1994)
Technology controls can work for decades. The USSR never caught up. But China is not the USSR.
