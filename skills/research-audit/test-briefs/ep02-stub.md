**WORKING TITLE:** The Rare Earth Bluff / 稀土博弈
**TARGET LENGTH:** 15-20 minutes
**HOOK:** China controls 70% of global rare earth mining and 90% of processing. In 2025, it weaponized that control. The world panicked. But the panic might be the point — because the geology says China's monopoly is a choice, not a fact.
**THESIS:** China's rare earth dominance is a strategic asset built on policy, not geology — and the history of commodity weaponization suggests it's a depreciating one.

## SECTION 1: NARRATIVE ARC

### Beat 1 — The Hook (0:00–2:00)
Open on China's rare earth export controls announcement. The world's reaction. Then the twist: rare earths aren't actually rare.

### Beat 2 — History (2:00–7:00)
How China built the monopoly. Deng Xiaoping's "Middle East has oil, China has rare earths" quote. The environmental cost other countries weren't willing to pay. Mountain Pass mine story.

### Beat 3 — The Weaponization (7:00–11:00)
2010 Japan incident. 2025 escalation. What China actually restricted and why.

### Beat 4 — The Response (11:00–15:00)
Western scramble. Australia, Canada, US projects. Recycling. Substitution research. How long does diversification actually take?

### Beat 5 — The Pattern (15:00–18:00)
Historical parallels to other commodity weaponizations. OPEC oil embargo. What happens when you weaponize a commodity — the supplier always loses long-term leverage.
