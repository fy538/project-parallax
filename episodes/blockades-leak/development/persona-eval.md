**Still watching? YES, DEEPLY ENGAGED**

Garrett Hardin's tragedy of the commons — this is the foundational framework for Sofia. "Individually rational, collectively catastrophic" is the core insight about prisoner's dilemma-type systems. The goat herder parable is the classic pedagogical form.

**Strongest moment so far:** "The enforcement mechanism itself creates the conditions for its own failure." This is Sofia's language. This is systems thinking. The script isn't saying "we're doing it wrong." It's saying "this structure contains a self-defeating loop."

**Strongest moment for Sofia:** "The unifying insight: In none of these cases — Venice, fishing quotas, NPT, OPEC — was the collapse due to insufficient enforcement... This is the moment where Sofia realizes the script has delivered on its promise: this isn't a policy problem, it's a *structural problem*.

"The pattern is structural, not accidental" — this is Sofia's favorite sentence in the entire script. Patterns are things you can apply elsewhere.
