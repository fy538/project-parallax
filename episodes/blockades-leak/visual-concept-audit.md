## Script: EP02 — Why Technological Blockades Always Leak (script-v1-production.md)
## Date: May 2, 2026

---

## Summary

EP02's visual layer is **structurally strong but operationally dense**. The script is 87% visually specified with excellent mode balance (FOOTAGE 55%, MG 33%, LAYERED 7%, matching targets exactly). The visual rhythm is deliberately alternating and builds clear patterns. However, the sheer **number of Remotion compositions (44 total) and the tight sequencing in Beats 4-5** create a production bottleneck: each template must be executed cleanly or the render chain breaks. Three moderate feasibility risks exist: (1) Beat 4 DataChart density may exceed viewer absorption, (2) stock footage search terms for "ASML EUV equipment" are ambiguous and may not source cleanly, (3) FrameworkDiagram count is high (12 total) and several attempt complex comparisons ("Murano vs Modern Controls," "OPEC vs Chip Controls") that risk rendering too densely.

**Verdict direction: VISUALLY READY with caveats** — all visuals are buildable with current tools and the script is well-executed. No script reshaping required. Production caution: the render complexity is real; this is manageable but not forgiving. If any template composition fails, fallback visual approaches are needed.

**Recommendation:** Use this restructuring. It preserves the script's intellectual power while improving visual rhythm. The two full cases (Venice + OPEC) are the most parallel to chips; the other two become evidence that supports the structure, not co-equal cases.
