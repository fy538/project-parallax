# ai-care-motives — ChatGPT prompts for the tier-3 pictures

> Rewritten 13 October 2026 for use in the episode. These prompts were first written on 11 October for the technique tests; the prompts themselves are unchanged, the order and the notes are new.
>
> **Why ChatGPT.** Pictures made from scratch on fal came out smoother and flatter than the twelve episode stills. Making these where the stills were made keeps the texture.
>
> **Face and body rules** in the opening message follow `project/PICTURE_RULES.md`: no facial parts from any angle, and anatomically coherent bodies. The older episode style block said "no realistic anatomy"; that phrase is dropped here on purpose.

## What each picture is for

| Order | Picture | Where it goes | What it replaces |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Three montage pictures: the start-up, the essay, the plan | Cold open, on "The startup. The essay. The plan you haven't told anyone yet." | Three close framings of the desk still |
| 2 | The street, very wide | Beat 4, on "Picture the street: other customers compare notes. Neighbors talk. A competitor would love the story." | The first part of the hub-and-spoke diagram. The diagram stays for "a fact about his street" |
| 3 | The car on the test rig | Beat 6, the Volkswagen section | To be placed when it exists: it is drawn in the shop's composition, so cutting to it echoes the shop |
| 4 | The model at the desk | Beat 7, on "Now return to the machine." | The laptop close-up |
| 5 (optional) | The shop with the street close | Beat 2, the second shopkeeper's window | The window framing, where the passers-by are small |

Make them in this order. The montage comes first because it changes the first ten seconds. If you only have twenty minutes, do rows 1 and 2.

## Session plan

- One ChatGPT conversation. About 40 minutes with regenerations for all seven images; about 20 for the first four.
- Save to `episodes/ai-care-motives/assets/stills-toolbox/` with the file names given under each shot. Keep rejected attempts too, named `...-v1.png`, `...-v2.png`.
- Tell me when they are there. I check every head and hand enlarged, upscale the pictures, build the changes on a copy of the manifest and show you a cut to compare, as with the last two passes.

## Check each picture before moving on

- **Heads:** no eyes, no nose bump on a profile, no mouth, nothing inside the ears.
- **Hands:** each one leads back to its own arm.
- **No lettering** anywhere, including on screens, signs and paper.
- **It sits beside the episode stills:** the same patterned cut-paper texture and the same amber and umber. If it comes back smooth or grey, ask again with: "Match the three episode stills exactly in texture and palette."

## Opening message (paste once, after the uploads)

```
I'm going to generate 7 more images for a video essay about trust, helpers, and artificial intelligence. I've uploaded reference images in two groups:

- First 5 images: Parallax channel style references. These lock the overall aesthetic that must be consistent across every image: constructivist editorial illustration, ink/amber/bone palette, angular color-blocked figures.
- Last 3 images: finished stills from this episode (a shopkeeper at a counter by a large window, a row of figures holding blank cards, a man at a desk at night). The new images must look like they were made in the same session as these three: the same faceted cut-paper texture, the same warm amber light and deep umber shadow, the same figure proportions.

Style rules that apply to every single image in this session:
- Constructivist editorial illustration: bold color-blocked forms, flat planes, no gradients, no soft airbrushed shading, no photorealism.
- NOT a realist tradition: never a naturalistic or photographic rendering, no rendered or expressive faces, no volumetric or photographic light. Warmth and mood come from PALETTE and light placement inside the flat constructivist register, never from switching art tradition.
- Bodies are anatomically coherent underneath the stylization: shoulders, elbows, wrists, hands and legs connect believably, each hand can be traced to its own arm, and every figure has clear support. Coherent structure, rendered in matte painted planes.
- Texture: the visible faceted, cut-paper grain of the three episode stills. Not smooth vector shapes.
- Palette locked to: ink #1C1814, amber #E5A544, bone #F0E6D0, with walnut #5C4A3D and grey-green earth tones where natural. No other hues.
- Figures: angular, color-blocked, mid-century modernist. Clothing color-blocked in ink with bone accents. Poses are still.
- Heads have NO facial parts, from any angle: no eyes, eyebrows or eye sockets; no nose, nostrils or nasal ridge; no mouth or lips; no lines or holes inside the ear. A head seen in profile has no nose or lip protrusion: the front of the face is one clean plane. A simple filled outer-ear shape is fine. Richness in a head comes from unequal painted planes of light and shadow, never from features. The same rule applies to shadows and reflections of heads.
- No decorative symbols, no icons, no equations, no glows, halos, rays or sunbursts.
- Text: none. No lettering, no numbers, no labels, no signs, no logos.
- Mood: quiet, human-scale, slightly austere, intelligent.

When I say "same composition as the shop still" I mean the first of the three episode stills: the same camera position and height, the same large window filling the left third of the picture, the same diagonal light across the floor. Only what I describe should change.
```

Same world and light as the desk-at-night still, 16:9. ## 2. The street, very wide (`toolbox-street.png`)

> Beat 4. The camera travels along it and each group arrives as it is named, so each group has to read on a phone.
> Ask for the widest format ChatGPT offers (3:1). If it returns 16:9, ask again for "an ultra-wide 3:1 panorama". A 16:9 version still works.

```
A new, very wide panoramic street scene, 3:1, seen straight on from across the street at eye level, like a long frieze read from left to right. It is the street outside the shop in the shop still: the same amber evening light and long shadows. Four clearly separated groups, with empty pavement between them:
1. Far left: the front of the small bakery with a large shop window. Inside, behind the counter, the same shopkeeper (dark hair, dark shirt with rolled sleeves, bone-white apron) with a loaf on the counter.
2. Left of centre: two customers standing on the pavement facing each other, each holding a wrapped loaf, heads inclined toward each other.
3. Right of centre: two neighbours talking across a low garden fence in front of a house.
4. Far right: the front of a second, rival bakery. Its owner stands in the doorway in an apron with arms crossed, looking down the street toward the first shop.
The figures are small in the frame, with plenty of plain wall and sky above them. Same faceted cut-paper texture as the episode stills. No shop signs, no lettering, no text.
```

No gauges with numbers. No text.
