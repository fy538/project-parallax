| This is the stag hunt. And it looks like the Prisoner's Dilemma — but it's fundamentally different. In the stag hunt, mutual cooperation *is* a Nash equilibrium. It's rational to cooperate — if you can trust that others will too. The puzzle isn't domination. It's coordination. | **P1** · [MG:] GameBoard — Side-by-side: PD matrix (one dot at mutual defection) vs. Stag Hunt matrix (two dots — one at mutual cooperation, one at mutual defection). | The philosopher Brian Skyrms proved something remarkable in 2004. {✅} An iterated Prisoner's Dilemma — the version people actually play in real life, where you meet the same counterpart repeatedly — is mathematically equivalent to a stag hunt.
