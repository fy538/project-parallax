## Why these prompts are different from v1

The v1 prompts produced **standalone scenes**. These v2 prompts produce **bridge frames** — the second half of a 2-frame morph. Two rules carry across all 12:

1. **Match the anchor frame's setting exactly** — same room, same lighting direction, same palette ratio, same figure clothing/proportions where figures persist. The morph tool will only reach the v2 frame from the v1 anchor if the two frames are spatially related.
2. **Direct the camera move, not the scene change** — most v2 frames are "the same scene from a different distance/angle." Tell ChatGPT what changed about the *frame*, not what changed about the *world*.
