## Counterargument Quality

### Steelman strength: Excellent.

Pass 1 Section 4 provides 7 distinct steelman arguments, escalating in sophistication:
1. PD predicts well where it fits (OPEC) — concedes domain
2. Pedagogical value is real — concedes utility
3. Schelling's "models are diagnostic tools" defense — the most sophisticated defense, engaged seriously
4. Gap-identification function — PD's failure IS informative
5. Mechanism design success vindicates methodology — $120B+ proof
6. Friedman's "as if" methodology — general defense of abstract modeling
7. Ostrom's boundary conditions — critics of the critic

A smart game theorist would recognize #3 and #5 as their strongest arguments, properly stated. This passes the "would an advocate recognize this as their best case?" test.

### Missing perspectives:

- **A practicing IR scholar who uses PD in classroom teaching** — their defense would be more practical ("it teaches strategic thinking even if it doesn't predict") than the theoretical steelmans. Partially covered by #2 but could be sharper.
- **A Chinese IR scholar** — how does the Chinese academy actually use game theory? The brief gestures at this via the 势/shi thread but doesn't have a Chinese defense of PD-style reasoning from within the Chinese tradition. Not critical for a Philosopher's Lens format but would enrich.

### Synthesis quality:

The thesis lands in a genuinely distinct position: "PD is a special case routinely misapplied as a general one, with measurable cost." This is NOT "both sides have a point" and NOT "PD is wrong." It reframes by identifying the specific failure mode (model colonization beyond validated domain) rather than attacking the model wholesale. The qualified form survives the steelman — exactly as designed.

### Epistemic discipline:

Language throughout is appropriately hedged. Key phrases used: "structural resonance," "suggests," "one reading of this is." The brief explicitly flags where evidence is weaker (Section on Methodology, "Where evidence is weaker / contested"). The script-level recommended phrasings in Pass 3 maintain this discipline. No overclaiming detected.
