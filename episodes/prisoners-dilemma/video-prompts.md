# Prisoners Dilemma — Video Prompts

> 17 image-to-video clips at 1080p. Upload each still and paste the motion prompt.
> Stills are in: `episodes/prisoners-dilemma/assets/stills/`
> All clips are 6 seconds (Hailuo free tier fixed duration). Trim or speed-ramp in NLE as needed.
>
> **All 17 shots go through image-to-video generation.** If a clip has unacceptable artifacts after 2 attempts, fall back to NLE Ken Burns with the same camera direction and duration.
>
> **Prompting rules:**
> - Only describe the motion you WANT. Don't mention things you want frozen — AI re-animates anything you name.
> - Keep prompts short, director-style. The model already sees the image — don't describe the scene.
> - Append negative prompt where supported: `-neg flicker, morphing, warping, text, watermark, letters, jitter`
> - Use low motion intensity for text-heavy or near-static shots.
> - Re-upload the image fresh for each new prompt attempt (may cache previous generations).
> - "Zoom-in" (scales image) ≠ "push-in" (generates new content). For text-heavy shots, zoom only.
