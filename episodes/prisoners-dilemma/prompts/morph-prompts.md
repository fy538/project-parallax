### Pair H4 ★ HERO — aigen-12a → aigen-13  [10s · HIGH · ★ HERO MORPH]

> Requires: `aigen-12a` in `public/` (Phase A still — ChatGPT Shot 25)

> **This is the single most important visual transition in the episode.** Run Kling 3.0 Pro
> (not Standard). Plan 3 generation attempts and pick the best. Do this last in the session
> when Hailuo is warmed up.

**Start frame:** `aigen-12a-alpine-pasture.png`
**End frame:** `aigen-13-ocean-horizon.png`
**Duration:** 10s

```
The stone walls and alpine terrain dissolve slowly outward. The compact stone commons expands
to an immense water horizon — meadow giving way to open ocean, the bounded cooperative space
becoming the unbounded challenge of global commons. Amber light holds across the transformation
as land becomes water. The cooperative logic that governed the commons is now confronted with
a scale it was not designed for.
```

Negative prompt:
```
-neg flicker, warping, camera move, watermark, text drift, figure appearing, buildings appearing, palette change, photoreal texture, animation artifacting, anime style
```

> What to watch (run 3× and compare):
> 1. **Stone wall dissolution:** do the walls dissolve organically, or pop out? Should feel like recession — walls becoming ocean horizon.
> 2. **Palette continuity:** the amber light should hold from land into water — both the alpine and ocean frames have amber warmth; the morph must preserve it.
> 3. **Horizon line:** the water horizon should emerge from the meadow horizon at the same elevation in frame.
> 4. **Duration distribution:** at 10s, the transformation should begin around 2s and complete around 8s — allowing 2s of settling. If the morph rushes at the start, that's a Hailuo pacing issue — note it and try a shorter duration or add a beat note.
>
> **Fallback if all 3 attempts fail:** hard cut between `aigen-12a` and `aigen-13` with a `color-wash` cut (ink, 0.5s each side). Document failure mode in `bakeoff/hero-morph-results.md`.
